# Book-derived research workflow

Source: *財報狗教你挖好股穩賺20%*, especially Part 6, `選股實戰》7大類存股好標的全面剖析`, printed pages 240–265. The source PDF is a historical method reference; its screenshots, dates, labels, and thresholds are not proof of the current Statement Dog UI or current market data.

## Eight-step sequence

| Step | Book default | What to verify now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recent one-year ROE > 15% and recent five-year average ROE > 15% | Use the current ROE/ROA screen controls and record both periods. |
| 2 | Recent one-year net margin > 10% and recent five-year average net margin > 10% | Use the current net-margin controls and record both periods. |
| 3 | Recent one-year operating cash flow/net profit > 50% and recent five-year average > 50% | Verify the ratio definition and period labels before applying it. |
| 4 | Recent one-year free cash flow per share > 0 and recent five-year average > 0 | Verify that the current control means per-share free cash flow. |
| 5 | List the screen results | Record result count, pagination, active conditions, and update state. |
| 6 | Industry analysis | Check long-term direction, durable competitive advantage, and the four burning-money warnings. |
| 7 | Dividend-discount return > 10%; use > 15% as a stricter depressed-market reference | Use the current valuation equivalent and label any mismatch. |
| 8 | Short-term revenue growth breaks above long-term revenue growth | Use the current short/long revenue-growth indicator and record the exact periods. |

The book's screenshots show the first four conditions being configured before `開始選股`, followed by industry analysis, valuation, and growth checks. The threshold values should remain configurable because the book is older than the current site data and UI.

## Industry warning checklist

For the book's “burning money” test, inspect whether the industry is characterized by:

1. Products or services that cannot be meaningfully differentiated.
2. Strong dependence on scale advantages.
3. Continued low-price technology research or competition.
4. Heavy government subsidies or spending across countries.

These are prompts for industry research, not automatic fail conditions. Explain why the evidence makes the company more or less exposed to each characteristic.

## Per-company order

Use the same four headings for every surviving company:

1. `獲利性分析`: long-term ROE, long-term net margin, industry value trend, and competitive advantage.
2. `安全性分析`: operating cash flow relative to net profit, free cash flow, debt/balance-sheet risks when relevant, and burning-money risk.
3. `價值評估`: dividend-discount or current equivalent, comparison with peers/industry, and why the valuation differs.
4. `成長性分析`: short-term versus long-term revenue growth and the industry/product/customer drivers behind it.

End with `投資方向`, but keep facts, interpretation, risks, and unknowns separate.
