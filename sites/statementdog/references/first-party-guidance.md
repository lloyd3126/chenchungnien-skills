# 財報狗第一方說明與方法論

## Data source and disclaimer

The site's footer and `/law/disclaimer` state that financial and analysis information is assembled from public sources including the Taiwan Stock Exchange, TPEx, and MOPS. If the site's information conflicts with an exchange, company, or official publication, use the official publication as the authority.

The disclaimer describes the site's investment information and seminars as auxiliary references, not a guarantee, recommendation, or promise of profit or loss reduction. It also warns that data can contain errors, omissions, delays, interruptions, or changes. Treat blog content as educational content rather than an investment basis.

## TAIEX explanation

The `/taiex` page defines TAIEX as a market-capitalization-weighted index compiled by TWSE, so higher-market-cap companies have greater influence. Its own explanation says absolute index level alone is not enough to judge high or low because inflation and GDP growth raise the level over time; it presents market P/B as a relative reference. Re-fetch the current P/B and index values when answering a current-market question.

## Quality-stock strategy

The `績優股清單` strategy explanation describes a sequence that:

1. Excludes companies whose free-cash-flow return is declining.
2. Selects companies with high three-year average free-cash-flow return.
3. Ranks by price-to-book ratio.
4. Ranks by price-to-earnings ratio.
5. Ranks by dividend yield.
6. Combines the rankings into the quality-stock list.

The same explanation defines free cash flow as operating cash flow minus capital expenditure and free-cash-flow return as free cash flow divided by invested capital. The historical-performance page reports backtested outcomes, but the period and values are dynamic; cite the current page and do not hard-code them.

## Site terminology

- `股票健診` is the site's grouped diagnostic view for categories such as avoiding financial landmines, dividend／stable stocks, growth, cheap valuation, ownership, quality and turnaround.
- `題材`／`概念股` pages explain why a company may benefit from a topic, classify benefit level or supply-chain role, and link to related news and tags. These are site-produced research explanations; distinguish them from official filings and current price data.
- `產業報告` pages organize a research report into sections, supply-chain maps and linked companies. Use the report for context and route to the company page for current metrics.
