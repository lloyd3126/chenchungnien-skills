# Seeking Alpha First-Party Guidance and Interpretation Limits

## Platform framing

The homepage describes Seeking Alpha as a financial research platform with analysis, market information, tools and a large investing community. Treat it as a research interface, not as a broker, trading instruction or guarantee of results.

## Article disclosures observed

Article pages visibly include an analyst disclosure and a Seeking Alpha disclosure. The disclosure states that past performance is not a guarantee of future results, that no recommendation or advice is being given about whether an investment is suitable, and that views expressed by third-party authors may not represent Seeking Alpha as a whole. It also states that analysts include professional and individual investors who may not be licensed or certified by an institute or regulatory body.

Some articles also include an `Editor's Note` about securities that do not trade on a major U.S. exchange. Preserve such warnings when they are visible and relevant.

## Interpretation contract

- Separate observable company/news facts, estimates, site-generated Quant／analyst／Wall Street ratings, third-party author opinion and the user's requested conclusion.
- Do not translate `Strong Buy`, `Buy`, `Hold`, `Sell` or a Quant grade into personal financial advice. Report the site's label, scope and observation time.
- When a company filing, earnings number or price matters, prefer the corresponding current symbol financials, SEC Filings, Press Releases or issuer/exchange source exposed by the page where available; retain source and date.
- Current charts, prices, estimates, rating grades, yields, article availability and news are time-sensitive. Re-fetch them for every task.
- If the site shows a registration or Premium gate, distinguish visible metadata from unavailable body or data and do not bypass it.

## Safety and source boundaries

- Do not expose account data, portfolio holdings, private alerts, credentials or session artefacts.
- Do not enter email, password, OTP, payment or other sensitive information into the site during exploration.
- Do not create, save, publish, follow, like, comment, subscribe, purchase or modify account state unless the user explicitly requests that exact action and the browser confirmation boundary is satisfied.
