# PR Newswire first-party guidance

## Platform terminology

- The Amplify platform page presents a lifecycle of `PLAN`, `CREATE`, `DISTRIBUTE`, and `REPORT`.
- The site describes AEO as Answer Engine Optimization and GEO as Generative Experience Optimization in its product marketing. Treat these as PR Newswire's marketing terminology, not as an independently verified ranking guarantee.
- The platform FAQ describes Multichannel Amplification as distributing content across digital, print, social, and direct-to-journalist channels. Preserve the wording as a first-party explanation and re-check the FAQ if the user asks for current product semantics.
- The site describes the platform as combining planning, content creation, distribution, and performance measurement. Product claims, network sizes, awards, and performance percentages are marketing claims that may change; cite the current page and do not hard-code them into routing instructions.

## RSS guidance

The first-party RSS page explains that RSS is an XML-based distribution format containing headlines, summaries, and links. It provides channel rows for all releases and visible categories, raw-feed controls, and guidance for journalists and web publishers. Use the current visible RSS button to obtain a feed; do not infer a feed URL from a label.

## Release semantics

Release timestamps are displayed with an ET label and should be treated as publication time shown by PR Newswire. Release bodies can contain company-supplied claims, forward-looking statements, legal disclaimers, or links to external organizations. Summarize what the release says and attribute claims; do not treat a press release as independent verification.

## Freshness and provenance

News rows, featured/most-viewed sections, resource lists, rankings, product claims, organization histories, RSS entries, and counts are dynamic. Re-fetch them in the current tab. For a current answer, report the page URL, visible title/timestamp, and the retrieval date when useful.

## Safety boundaries

The public site exposes account creation, demo/contact forms, contractual checkboxes, marketing-consent language, and reCAPTCHA. These forms transmit personal or organizational data and may create sales/account records. Keep them at inspection only unless the user explicitly requests the exact submission and confirms at the action boundary. Do not solve CAPTCHA, share private data, or enter credentials.
